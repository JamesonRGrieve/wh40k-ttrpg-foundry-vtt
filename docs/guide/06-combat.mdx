# Combat

## Start an encounter

Add tokens to Foundry's combat tracker and start the encounter.

- **Initiative**: the initiative action on the **Combat** tab opens **Roll Initiative**, which accepts a situational modifier and **Burn a Fate Point (take 10 instead of 1d10)**.
- **Actions**: each combatant has a **Full**, **Half**, **Free** and **Reaction** budget in the combat tracker. It resets each turn.
- **Attacks need an encounter**: a weapon attack requires a running encounter that includes the attacker's token. The GM can disable **Require Active Combat to Attack**.

## Movement

With **Movement Automation** at **Full Automation** (default), during an encounter:

- Players move their own token only on its turn.
- A move cannot exceed the distance left for the chosen type (Half, Full, Charge, Run). The Combat tab shows what is left.
- The GM moves tokens freely.

## Make an attack

1. **Draw Weapon** on the Combat tab. Only equipped weapons attack.
2. Target the enemy token.
3. Attack from the sheet, or click **Attack** on the weapon's chat card.

A jammed, empty or recharging weapon does not fire. Use **Reload** or **Clear Jam** (see [Items and Effects](?path=/docs/guide-items-and-effects--docs)).

### Attack options

- **Attack Mode** (ranged): Standard Attack, Semi-Auto, Full-Auto, Suppressing Fire, Overwatch, Called Shot, as the weapon allows.
- **Attack Mode** (melee): Standard Attack, Charge, All-Out Attack, Guarded Attack, Called Shot, Feint, Knockdown, Stun, Manoeuvre and Disarm.
- **Aim**: No Aim, Half Aim, Full Aim.
- **Range** and **target size**: set from the target; you can override both. Range bands are Point Blank, Short, Standard, Long and Extreme.
- **Situational**: for example Prone, Unaware Target, Engaged in Melee, Darkness, High Ground, Stunned Target, Helpless Target, fog or smoke, cover and Ganging Up.
- **Firing options**: weapon qualities you can opt into for this attack.

**Cover** adds armour at the hit location instead of penalising the roll. **Auto Line of Sight & Cover** pre-selects cover from scene walls. **Higher Ground elevation band** sets the height needed for the High Ground bonus.

### Hit location

On a hit, the attack roll's digits are reversed (23 becomes 32) to find the hit location, shown on the card.

### Dodge and Parry

When an attack hits a target, the defender's owners and the GM get a whispered **Evasion** prompt: **Dodge**, **Parry** or **Take the hit**. Nobody else can answer it. Damage waits until the defender answers.

- The test is the defender's own skill test, with every modifier listed. It spends their Reaction.
- A success negates the hit. Against a burst or other multi-hit attack, each extra degree negates one more hit, as the line prints. Negated hits are shown on the attack card, and a fully evaded attack has no damage to roll.
- **Parry** is closed against ranged attacks, Flexible weapons, and a defender holding only Unwieldy weapons. The parrying weapon's Balanced, Defensive or Unbalanced quality modifies the test, and a Fast attacking weapon adds its penalty.
- A defender with no Reaction left, a condition that removes it, or in a grapple cannot react.
- **Counter Attack**: after a successful Parry, a character with the Counter Attack talent gets **Counter attack** on the card. It attacks with the parrying weapon at the talent's penalty. In Dark Heresy 2e it is once per turn.

### Grapples

An unarmed **Charge** or **Standard Attack** can **Begin Grapple**. While grappled, a **Grapple** panel on the Combat tab gives each side its line's own options, such as **Damage Opponent**, **Throw Down Opponent**, **Push Opponent** (the GM moves the tokens), **Take Control**, **Ready** or **Use Item** (pick the item), **Stand**, **Slip Free** and **Break Free**. Break Free ends the grapple for both. Grapple participants cannot use Reactions.

### Spray, Blast and grenades

- A **Spray** or **Blast** attack places a template: a cone from the firer, or a circle on the target that scatters on a miss. Drag or rotate it, then press **Resolve Template Targets** on the attack card. Spray makes no attack roll: everyone in the cone tests Agility (Dodge with the Leaping Dodge talent), and each failure is a Body hit. Blast hits everyone inside. Each hit goes to the damage card for its own target.
- A grenade with a rider (smoke, stun, gas and similar) opens **Throw Grenade**. Place its area, then press **Resolve Rider**: everyone caught makes the rider's test and failures gain its condition. A rider grenade that also deals damage rolls it through the normal weapon roll.

## Damage

With **Auto-roll Damage on Hit** (default on), a hit posts a damage card. Otherwise click **Roll Damage** on the attack card.

Each hit shows:

- hit number, location, and target name when hits are spread across enemies;
- **Formula**, **Base Damage** and each named modifier;
- total, damage type and **Penetration**;
- **Effective** damage after armour (less penetration) and Toughness Bonus, when a target is selected;
- weapon effects and Righteous Fury results;
- **Replace die with DoS**: swap one damage die for the attack's Degrees of Success. Unavailable after a page reload.

A weapon that deals no damage (stun, smoke, webbers and similar) shows **No Damage** instead of a damage button; its qualities still apply. Some weapons take their damage from the range band, a roll table or another weapon; the card names the source. A Warp Weapon ignores armour and cover unless warded or holy armour covers the location.

### Righteous Fury

A damage die showing 10 triggers Righteous Fury. Some qualities, such as Vengeful or Gauss, lower the threshold. Each trigger lists its critical effect, with text from your content packs. A Righteous Fury hit that defences would fully absorb still deals 1 damage, shown as **Absorbed (1)**.

### Apply damage

Click **Assign Damage** on the damage card. The system:

1. reduces the location's armour by penetration, to a minimum of 0;
2. subtracts that armour and the Toughness Bonus from the damage;
3. removes the rest from wounds;
4. treats damage beyond remaining wounds as critical damage, rolls the critical effect and applies its conditions.

- **Assign Damage** on the Combat tab applies damage and fatigue by hand.
- With **Auto-apply Damage to Target**, GM-rolled damage against a selected target applies automatically ("Damage applied automatically"). Player-rolled damage still uses the button.
- A Deathwatch horde loses Magnitude instead of wounds.

## Vehicles and ships

- **Vehicles**: hits use the facing armour the attack strikes, then Structural Integrity, and critical damage rolls on the vehicle critical charts. Crew tests take the vehicle's trait modifiers.
- **Rogue Trader voidcraft**: fire from the **Weapons** tab; firing a macrobattery offers the ship's other macrobatteries to combine into one salvo. Actions with an authored effect have a **Roll** button: the card shows the test and, on a success, applies the effect, which then appears under **Ship Actions in Effect**.
- **Ship Assault Actions** (Rogue Trader): **Ram**, **Board** and **Boarding Round** on **Manoeuvres**, **Hit and Run** on **Actions**. One dialog picks the target ship, rolls each test with its modifiers, asks the winner to choose, and applies the result when you own the target ship. Otherwise the card tells the GM.
- A ship at 0 Hull Integrity is **Crippled**: −10 Manoeuvrability and Detection, its weapons' Strength is halved, and damage past its Armour becomes a Critical Hit. It is **Destroyed** only by a Critical Hit result. The sheet shows the state under Hull Integrity.

## Psychic powers

Use **Invoke Power** or **Attack Power** in the Psychic Powers panel. Choose the power and level:

- **Fettered**: half PR, no phenomena.
- **Unfettered**: full PR, phenomena on doubles.
- **Push**: PR plus **Push Level**, always triggers phenomena.

With **Auto-roll Psychic Phenomena** (default on), phenomena draw from the Psychic Phenomena table, and severe results continue to Perils of the Warp. Both tables come from your content packs.

- **Sanctic Purity**: when Phenomena triggers on a Sanctic Daemonology power, a psyker with the talent may spend a Fate Point to negate it.
- **Malefic Daemonology** (Dark Heresy 2e): a successful cast costs Corruption equal to the Psy Rating used, shown on the card. Gaining Corruption can queue a Malignancy Test.

For thin-veiled scenes, see **Warp Weakness** in [Other Tools](?path=/docs/guide-other-tools--docs).

## Conditions and effects

- Conditions appear in the Token HUD status list, built from the condition items in your content packs for the **Primary Game System**, plus **Dead**. Only the GM adds conditions.
- With **Active Effect Processing** (default on), ongoing effects such as burning or blood loss resolve when the turn changes.
- **Fatigue** follows each line's rules unless the GM sets **Fatigue Model**.
- With **Dead tokens become lootable piles** and the Item Piles module, a creature marked **Dead** drops its carried gear as a pile.
- Conditions such as burning, bleeding or stunned are drawn on the tokens that bear them.

## Animations and sounds

Attacks play on the canvas the way they resolved: shots and impacts per hit, misses, jams, overheats, area templates, melee swings, Dodges and Parries, psychic powers, Phenomena, Perils and criticals. Weapons, powers and conditions play the sounds their content carries; the system ships a set of CC0 sounds for them to use. The GM turns all of this off for everyone with **Canvas animations and sounds**. Each player has their own animation, sound, volume and condition settings. Reduced-motion systems get no attack animations, and conditions are shown still.
