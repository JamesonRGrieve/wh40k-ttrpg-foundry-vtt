# The Character Sheet

Each game line has its own sheet, but they share the same layout: a sidebar with the portrait and identity on the left, and tabs on the right. Hover over a tab to see a short description of what it contains.

## Sidebar

| Element | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Portrait | Click to choose an image. |
| d20 icon | Opens the Origin Path Builder (tooltip **Setup Origin Path**). See [Creating a Character](?path=/docs/guide-creating-a-character--docs). |
| **Generate portrait (AI)** | GM only. Generates a portrait through the image service configured in the system settings. |
| **Re-roll portrait** / **Pin this portrait** | GM only, and only when the actor has a pool of several portraits to choose from. |
| Name and player | Editable name and player name fields. |

The window's header also has a **Reset Window Size** control.

## Tabs

| Tab | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Vitals, active effects, resources, favourite skills and talents, experience, and line-specific panels. |
| **Statistics** | Characteristics, aptitudes, skills, specialist skills, talents and traits. |
| **Combat** | The combat station: vitals, Fate Points, armour by location, weapons, movement, combat actions and psychic powers. |
| **Inventory** | Carried gear and gear stored elsewhere, with weights. |
| **Biography** | Identity details, appearance, personality and background notes, contacts and adversaries, and a character journal. |

### Overview

- **Active Effects**: each effect shows its remaining duration where it has one. Use the controls to enable, disable or delete an effect.
- **Resources**: the line's currencies, each with a stepper. In Dark Heresy 2e this shows **Influence**, **Requisition** and **Throne Gelt**, depending on the **DH2e Economy Ruleset** setting. In Homebrew mode, Influence has its own roll button here.
- **Party Overview** (Dark Heresy 2e): the shared warband **Subtlety** pool. A change made on one acolyte's sheet updates every open sheet.
- **Favourite skills and talents**: anything you starred on the Statistics tab, for quick access.
- **Experience**: total, spent and available XP, rank, and an itemised list of **XP Purchases**. Click **Advance** to buy advances. The GM also sees **Add XP**.
- Clicking the Fate Point icon or label opens **Uses of Fate Points**, a reference list of what a Fate Point can do.

Line-specific panels also appear on the Overview tab:

| Line | Panels |
| --- | --- |
| Black Crusade | Chaos Alignment, Psychic Strength, Chaos Ritual, Gifts of the Gods, Supplement Mechanics, Apotheosis |
| Deathwatch | Adeptus Astartes, Distinctions, Special-Issue Ammunition, Cohesion, Combat Mode, Renown, Requisition, Mission Oath, Mission, Vehicle |
| Only War | Regiment, Craftsmanship, Regimental Drawback, Comrade, Squad Logistics, Orders, Mission Assignment Gear, Vehicle Movement, Comrade Healing, Mount, Battlefield Awareness |

Some panels only appear when they apply, for example **Possession** while a character is possessed and **Grapple** while a grapple is in progress.

### Statistics

- Click a characteristic or skill to open the roll dialog. See [Rolling Tests](?path=/docs/guide-rolling-tests--docs).
- Filter the skill list by name, and star skills and talents to add them to the Overview favourites.
- Add specialisations for skills that take one from the specialist skills panel.
- Talents and traits are listed with their details. Trait levels can be adjusted.

### Combat

The combat station collects everything you need on your turn:

- **Vitals** and **Fate** (spend and restore Fate Points).
- **Armour** by hit location.
- **Weapons**: each equipped weapon is marked. Use the hand button to **Draw Weapon** or **Holster Weapon**. A hand budget shows how many hands your drawn weapons use.
- **Movement**: Half, Full, Charge and Run distances, plus lift and push/drag capacity. During combat it also shows the movement remaining this turn.
- **Combat Actions**: action buttons grouped as Half, Full and Reaction actions, plus **Dodge**, **Parry**, initiative and **Assign Damage**. Click an action for a description; Shift+Click posts it to chat.
- **Psychic Powers**: invoke or attack with a power, with your Psy Rating shown.

See [Combat](?path=/docs/guide-combat--docs) for how attacks and damage work.

### Inventory

Items are split into **Carried** and **Elsewhere**, with weights and an encumbrance warning. Each item has buttons to edit, equip, use, delete, or **Drop on the ground** (which creates a loot pile at your token). Tick several items and use **Give selected items to another character** to hand them over.

### Biography

Fill in identity and appearance fields, longer notes such as background, personality, superstitions and treasured mementos, and the **Contacts** and **Adversaries** lists. Age can be shown as biological or standard age. The **Character Journal** keeps journal entries on the character.

## NPC sheets

NPC sheets use the same layout with these tabs: **Skills**, **Combat**, **Inventory**, **Biography** and **NPC**. The NPC tab holds GM-facing controls such as hordes. NPCs open on the Skills tab.

By default an NPC is a flat stat block. With the **NPCs advance like characters** setting, its stats are treated as purchased advances so you can keep advancing it.

## Vehicle sheets

Land vehicles and aircraft use the vehicle sheet, with **Overview**, **Combat**, **Crew**, **Components** and **Notes** tabs.

## Voidcraft sheets (Rogue Trader)

Voidcraft use the starship sheet, with **Stats**, **Components**, **Weapons**, **Crew**, **History**, **Actions** and **Manoeuvres** tabs.

## Loot piles

A loot pile is a special actor created when someone drops an item on the ground. Right-click the pile's token and use **Pick Up**, or open the pile to take individual items. Players cannot move loot piles.

## Hotbar macros

Drag an item from a character sheet onto Foundry's macro hotbar to create a macro that rolls or uses it.
