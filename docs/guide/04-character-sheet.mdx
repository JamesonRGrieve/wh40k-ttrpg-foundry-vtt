# The character sheet

Every line's sheet has a sidebar and tabs. Hover a tab for a description. The header has **Reset Window Size**.

## Sidebar

- **Portrait**: click to choose an image.
- **d20 icon**: opens the Origin Path Builder (**Setup Origin Path**). See [Creating a Character](?path=/docs/guide-creating-a-character--docs).
- **Generate portrait (AI)**: GM only. Uses the image service in [Game Settings](?path=/docs/guide-game-settings--docs).
- **Re-roll portrait** / **Pin this portrait**: GM only, when the actor has a pool of portraits.
- **Name** and **player** fields.

## Overview

- **Active Effects**: remaining duration per effect; enable, disable or delete.
- **Resources**: the line's currencies, each with a stepper. Dark Heresy 2e shows **Influence**, **Requisition** and **Throne Gelt** according to **DH2e Economy Ruleset**. In Homebrew mode Influence has a roll button.
- **Party Overview** (Dark Heresy 2e): the shared warband **Subtlety** pool. A change on one sheet updates every open sheet.
- **Favourites**: skills and talents you starred on the Statistics tab.
- **Experience**: total, spent and available XP, rank, and **XP Purchases**. **Advance** buys advances. The GM also sees **Add XP**.
- **Fate Points**: click the icon or label for **Uses of Fate Points**.

Line-specific panels:

- **Black Crusade**: Chaos Alignment, Psychic Strength, Chaos Ritual, Gifts of the Gods, Supplement Mechanics, Apotheosis.
- **Deathwatch**: Adeptus Astartes, Distinctions, Special-Issue Ammunition, Cohesion, Combat Mode, Renown, Requisition, Mission Oath, Mission, Vehicle.
- **Only War**: Regiment, Craftsmanship, Regimental Drawback, Comrade, Squad Logistics, Orders, Mission Assignment Gear, Vehicle Movement, Comrade Healing, Mount, Battlefield Awareness.

Some panels appear only when relevant, such as **Possession** and **Grapple**.

## Statistics

Characteristics, aptitudes, skills, specialist skills, talents and traits.

- Click a characteristic or skill to roll. See [Rolling Tests](?path=/docs/guide-rolling-tests--docs).
- Filter skills by name. Star skills and talents to add them to Overview.
- Add specialisations in the specialist skills panel.
- Adjust trait levels.

## Combat

- **Vitals** and **Fate**: spend and restore Fate Points.
- **Armour** by hit location.
- **Weapons**: **Draw Weapon** / **Holster Weapon** on the hand button. A hand budget shows hands in use.
- **Movement**: Half, Full, Charge and Run distances, lift and push/drag capacity, and movement left this turn during combat.
- **Combat Actions**: Half, Full and Reaction actions, plus **Dodge**, **Parry**, initiative and **Assign Damage**. Click an action for its description; Shift+Click posts it to chat.
- **Psychic Powers**: invoke or attack with a power; shows Psy Rating.

See [Combat](?path=/docs/guide-combat--docs).

## Inventory

Items are split into **Carried** and **Elsewhere**, with weights and an encumbrance warning. Each item can be edited, equipped, used, deleted or **Drop on the ground** (creates a loot pile at your token). Tick several items and use **Give selected items to another character** to hand them over.

## Biography

Identity and appearance, notes (background, personality, superstitions, treasured mementos), **Contacts**, **Adversaries** and the **Character Journal**. Age displays as biological or standard age.

## Other actor sheets

- **NPC**: tabs **Skills** (opens first), **Combat**, **Inventory**, **Biography** and **NPC** (GM controls such as hordes). An NPC is a flat stat block unless **NPCs advance like characters** is on.
- **Vehicle** (land vehicles, aircraft): **Overview**, **Combat**, **Crew**, **Components**, **Notes**.
- **Voidcraft** (Rogue Trader): **Stats**, **Components**, **Weapons**, **Crew**, **History**, **Actions**, **Manoeuvres**.
- **Loot pile**: created when an item is dropped on the ground. Right-click its token and use **Pick Up**, or open it to take single items. Players cannot move loot piles.

## Hotbar macros

Drag an item from a sheet to the macro hotbar to create a macro that rolls or uses it.
