# Items, Equipment and Effects

Almost everything a character has is an item: weapons, armour, gear, ammunition, cybernetics, force fields, talents, traits, skills, psychic powers, conditions, critical injuries and origin paths. Each type has its own item sheet.

## Item sheets and edit mode

Item sheets open read-only. If you are allowed to edit the item, use the edit toggle on the sheet (**Enter Edit Mode** / **Exit Edit Mode**). Items inside a compendium cannot be edited from the sheet.

Some items are locked even in your own world unless the GM enables **Freeform Character Editing**:

- special abilities, malignancies, mutations and mental disorders;
- a character's own copy of a weapon or origin path. Copies in the world's Items sidebar or in a compendium are not affected.

These items are meant to be maintained in their compendium. A change made there reaches every actor that uses the item.

### Compendium-linked items

Items on an actor stay linked to their compendium entry. With **Hydrate Embedded Items From Compendiums on World Boot** on (the default), each item's description and rules are joined from the compendium when the world loads. Your per-character details, such as skill advances, ammo counts, equipped state, modifications, quantities and XP cost, are kept. A compendium edit therefore reaches every actor on the next load. Sheets also refresh this data whenever they open.

## How items change your rolls

Talents, traits, gear, conditions and effects can all modify tests, damage, penetration and more. The system collects these modifiers in one place, so each one shows on the chat card as a named row with its source. Hover over a row to see where it came from. See [Rolling Tests](?path=/docs/guide-rolling-tests--docs).

Modifiers reach a roll in three ways:

| Kind | Where you see it | Can you switch it off? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always-on | **Passive Effects** in the roll dialog, already included in the base value | No. These are listed for transparency only. |
| Situational | Pills in the roll dialog, from talents, traits and conditions | Yes. Click to apply when the situation fits. |
| Gear | **Gear** chips in the roll dialog, from equipment you carry | Yes. Click to say you are using the item. |

Some modifiers scale with something about the roll, such as a characteristic bonus or Degrees of Success, or only apply in certain cases, such as on a critical hit. These values come from the item's data in the content packs; the system works them out when you roll.

### Active Effects

Foundry Active Effects on an actor show on the **Active Effects** panel on the Overview and Combat tabs, with their remaining duration. Enable, disable or delete them from there. Durations count down as the GM advances in-game time.

## Weapons

The weapon sheet shows the weapon's profile: class, damage, penetration, range, rate of fire, clip and reload. It has tabs for description, modifications and notes.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reload** | Reload a weapon carried by a character. |
| **Clear Jam** | Shown when the weapon is jammed. After clearing, the weapon must be reloaded. |
| **Build Clip** | Load several ammunition types in firing order, for example 3 AP rounds then 2 Hot-shot. Only shown when the GM enables **Enable Magazine Building**. |
| Ammunition | Load or eject special ammunition by dragging it onto the weapon. |
| Modifications | Drag weapon modifications onto the weapon. Each can be switched on or off. |
| Qualities | Click a quality to open its entry. |
| Roll attack / damage | Start an attack or damage roll from the sheet. |

**Craftsmanship** (Poor, Cheap, Common, Good, Best and so on) is shown on the sheet.

**Unidentified weapons**: in edit mode the GM can untick **Identified** on a weapon. Players then see **???** in place of its stats; the GM always sees the real values.

**Bound items**: an item marked as bound cannot be dropped or traded.

### Weapon qualities

Weapon qualities are compendium items. Each quality's game effect is stored as data on the quality, and the system applies it automatically during attack and damage rolls. For example, a quality can lower the Righteous Fury threshold or change how damage dice are rolled. Qualities that apply to an attack are listed under **Active Qualities** on the attack card. Qualities you may choose to use appear under **Firing options** in the roll dialog.

## Armour and force fields

Armour gives protection by hit location, shown on the Combat tab. Force fields have their own roll; the roll dialog shows whether the field is **Activated**, **Not Activated** or **Overloaded**.

## Carrying, dropping and giving items

See the Inventory section of [The Character Sheet](?path=/docs/guide-the-character-sheet--docs): items can be carried or stored elsewhere, dropped on the ground as a loot pile, or given to another character you control.
