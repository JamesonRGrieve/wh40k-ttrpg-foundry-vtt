# Items, equipment and effects

Weapons, armour, gear, ammunition, cybernetics, force fields, talents, traits, skills, psychic powers, conditions, critical injuries and origin paths are all items. Each type has its own sheet.

## Editing items

Item sheets open read-only. Use **Enter Edit Mode** / **Exit Edit Mode** if you can edit the item. Compendium items cannot be edited from the sheet.

Unless **Freeform Character Editing** is on, these are also locked:

- special abilities, malignancies, mutations and mental disorders;
- a character's own copy of a weapon or origin path. Copies in the Items sidebar or a compendium are unaffected.

Maintain these in their compendium; a change there reaches every actor using the item.

### Compendium-linked items

Items on an actor stay linked to their compendium entry. With **Hydrate Embedded Items From Compendiums on World Boot** (default on), descriptions and rules are joined from the compendium at world load, and again when a sheet opens. Per-character details are kept: skill advances, ammo, equipped state, modifications, quantities and XP cost.

## How items modify rolls

Talents, traits, gear, conditions and effects can modify tests, damage, penetration and more. Each modifier appears on the chat card as a named row with its source.

| Kind | Shown as | Toggle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always-on | **Passive Effects**, already in the base | No |
| Situational | Pills from talents, traits and conditions | Yes |
| Gear | **Gear** chips from carried equipment | Yes |

Some modifiers scale with the roll (a characteristic bonus, Degrees of Success) or apply only in some cases (a critical hit). The values come from the item's content-pack data and are evaluated at roll time.

### Active Effects

Active Effects appear in the **Active Effects** panel on Overview and Combat, where you enable, disable or delete them. Durations count down as the GM advances in-game time.

## Weapons

The sheet shows class, damage, penetration, range, rate of fire, clip, reload and **Craftsmanship**, with description, modifications and notes tabs.

- **Reload**: reload a weapon a character carries.
- **Clear Jam**: shown when jammed. Reload afterwards.
- **Build Clip**: load several ammunition types in firing order, for example 3 AP then 2 Hot-shot. Requires **Enable Magazine Building**.
- **Ammunition**: load or eject special ammunition by dragging it onto the weapon.
- **Modifications**: drag modifications onto the weapon; toggle each on or off.
- **Qualities**: click a quality to open it.
- Start an attack or damage roll from the sheet.

**Unidentified weapons**: in edit mode the GM can untick **Identified**. Players then see **???** in place of stats; the GM sees the real values.

**Bound items** cannot be dropped or traded.

### Weapon qualities

Qualities are compendium items whose effect is stored as data and applied automatically to attack and damage rolls, for example lowering the Righteous Fury threshold. Applied qualities are listed under **Active Qualities** on the attack card. Optional ones appear under **Firing options** in the roll dialog.

## Armour and force fields

Armour protects by hit location, shown on the Combat tab. Force fields have their own roll, which reports **Activated**, **Not Activated** or **Overloaded**.

For carrying, dropping and giving items, see [The Character Sheet](?path=/docs/guide-the-character-sheet--docs).
