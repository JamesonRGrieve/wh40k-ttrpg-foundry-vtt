# Items, equipment and effects

Weapons, armour, gear, ammunition, cybernetics, force fields, talents, traits, skills, psychic powers, conditions, critical injuries, origin paths, Dark Pacts and medals are all items. Each type has its own sheet.

## Editing items

Item sheets open read-only. Use **Enter Edit Mode** / **Exit Edit Mode** if you can edit the item. Compendium items cannot be edited from the sheet.

In edit mode a sheet edits every field the item stores, grouped in sections below its description. That includes the structured rules the rolls read: a talent's or trait's re-roll rules, a psychic power's target, Focus Power test, range and phenomena modifier, a skill's alternative characteristics and example difficulties, a Dark Pact's Corruption, terms, Boons and Banes, and a weapon modification's changes to the weapon. Lists have add and remove buttons. Every item also has an **Acquired** note. Navigator powers, oaths, orders, rituals, ship roles, backpacks and vehicle traits and upgrades each have their own sheet. A Navigator power's mastery stays editable outside edit mode, because a Navigator raises it in play.

Unless **Freeform Character Editing** is on, these are also locked:

- special abilities, malignancies, mutations and mental disorders;
- a character's own copy of a weapon or origin path. Copies in the Items sidebar or a compendium are unaffected.

Maintain these in their compendium; a change there reaches every actor using the item.

### Compendium-linked items

Items on an actor stay linked to their compendium entry. With **Hydrate Embedded Items From Compendiums on World Boot** (default on), descriptions and rules are joined from the compendium at world load, and again when a sheet opens. Per-character details are kept: skill advances, ammo, equipped state, modifications, quantities and XP cost.

## How items modify rolls

Talents, traits, gear, conditions and effects can modify tests, damage, penetration and more. Each modifier appears on the chat card as a named row with its source.

| Kind | Shown as | Toggle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always-on | **Passive Effects**, already in the base | No |
| Situational | Pills from talents, traits and conditions | Yes |
| Gear | **Gear** chips from carried equipment | Yes |

Some modifiers scale with the roll (a characteristic bonus, Degrees of Success) or apply only in some cases (a critical hit). The values come from the item's content-pack data and are evaluated at roll time.

### Active Effects

Active Effects appear in the **Active Effects** panel on Overview and Combat, where you enable, disable or delete them. Durations count down as the GM advances in-game time.

## Weapons

The sheet shows class, damage, penetration, range, rate of fire, clip, reload and **Craftsmanship**, with description, modifications and notes tabs.

- **Reload**: reload a weapon a character carries. With **Track standard ammunition (Homebrew)** on, each starting ranged weapon comes with two clips of its standard ammunition, and Reload uses them up; off, Reload tops the weapon up without one.
- **Clear Jam**: shown when jammed. Reload afterwards.
- **Build Clip**: load several ammunition types in firing order, for example 3 AP then 2 Hot-shot. Requires **Enable Magazine Building**.
- **Ammunition**: load or eject special ammunition by dragging it onto the weapon.
- **Modifications**: toggle each fitted modification on or off. On a character's own weapon, **Fit a modification** fits one the character carries, with the line's test: in Dark Heresy 2e a Trade (Armourer) test at the modification's Availability, where failure loses the parts and 2+ degrees of failure make the weapon Unreliable. **Remove and keep the parts (test)** takes it off again. Lines with no printed test fit it freely. A fitted modification can change damage, penetration, to-hit, range (added, then multiplied), clip size, rate of fire and weight. The weapon sheet lists each change, and the weapon uses the new values for attacks, Reload and clip building. A modification's sheet edits those changes, and an edit reaches weapons it is already fitted to.
- **Repair**: the Tech-Use Repair use fixes a broken item. In Dark Heresy 2e it takes the item's craftsmanship modifier, and failure by 4+ degrees destroys the item. Xenos gear needs a Forbidden Lore test first, if the GM rules the maker is known.
- **Qualities**: click a quality to open it. In edit mode, choose one from your line's weapon qualities and select **Add Quality**, or drop a quality onto the sheet. A quality marked (X), such as Toxic (X), needs its level in the box beside the list. The cross on a quality removes it. Qualities that come from craftsmanship, ammunition or a firing mode have no cross; change their source instead.
- Start an attack or damage roll from the sheet. Melee weapons, and thrown weapons other than grenades, add the wielder's Strength Bonus to their damage. The damage tile shows this as **+SB**, and the damage card lists it as its own line.

**Thrown range**: a range printed as a multiple of a characteristic bonus (SBx3, or WPBx5 for a power) is worked out from the thrower's bonus at roll time.

**Unidentified weapons**: in edit mode the GM can untick **Identified**. Players then see **???** in place of stats; the GM sees the real values.

**Bound items** cannot be dropped or traded.

## Mutations, Gifts and special abilities

- A Black Crusade **Gift of the Gods** is a mutation with the Gift category. Its sheet holds the base effect, a rider per Chaos alignment, granted traits and talents, a sub-table and any rolled characteristic loss.
- A Deathwatch **Distinction**, **Mark of Distinction** or **Advanced Special Ability** is a special ability with that category. Its sheet holds the Renown rank it needs, its Renown reward and XP cost, a Distinction's Marks, and a Mark's grant.
- A **Dark Pact** holds the Daemon's terms, the Corruption it costs, and its Boons and Banes.
- A **medal** (Only War) holds what it is awarded for and its Squad Bonus.

### Weapon qualities

Qualities are compendium items whose effect is stored as data and applied automatically to attack and damage rolls, for example lowering the Righteous Fury threshold. Applied qualities are listed under **Active Qualities** on the attack card. Optional ones appear under **Firing options** in the roll dialog.

## Armour and force fields

Armour protects by hit location, shown on the Combat tab. Drop an armour modification on an armour sheet to fit it: it must suit the armour type and have a free slot of its kind. Power-armour sub-systems use **Sub-system Slots** (Black Crusade power armour holds three); a fitted modification can add slots and change the armour's protection. The GM is told why a modification was refused. Force fields have their own roll, which reports **Activated**, **Not Activated** or **Overloaded**.

## Auspexes and scanners

An auspex gives its usual Awareness bonus while you carry it. **Use** it from your inventory to scan: your character's token must be on the scene. The scan rolls its Tech-Use test with the usual roll card. On a success a ring sweeps out from your token to the scanner's range, and each creature or vehicle it picks up shows as a blip for a few seconds. Only you and the GM see the blips, and they don't reveal hidden tokens. The GM also gets a private card listing each contact by name, distance, bearing and whether it reads as a life sign or a machine. A failed test reads nothing.

The scanner's own data sets its range and how thick a wall it sees through, so each line's auspex works as its rulebook prints it. In Dark Heresy 2e a scan is limited to once a round in combat, and a Poor auspex sees through less. Foundry walls have no thickness, so the GM marks a wall in its **Wall Configuration** under **Auspex Scans**: a **Thickness** in centimetres, or **Blocks scanners** for a shielding material. Unmarked walls count as ordinary thin walls and hide nothing from a scan.
