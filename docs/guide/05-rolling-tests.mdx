# Rolling tests

Every test uses the same **roll dialog** and posts a chat card with the full calculation. Open it by clicking a characteristic or skill on **Statistics**, or a weapon or power on **Combat**.

## Target and difficulty

The large number is the **final target**: roll equal to or under it on d100 to succeed. A 1 always succeeds; a 100 always fails. Click the difficulty button to change difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Trivial | +60 |
| Elementary | +50 |
| Simple | +40 |
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging (default) | +0 |
| Difficult | −10 |
| Hard | −20 |
| Very Hard | −30 |
| Arduous | −40 |
| Punishing | −50 |
| Hellish | −60 |

The line under the target shows **Base**, difficulty, **Mods** (situational) and **Custom**. Total modifiers are capped at ±60.

## Modifier sections

Only sections that apply to the test appear.

- **Skill uses**: pick the use you are attempting. Some uses ask for a target.
- **Test Variant**: a sense channel (Visual, Auditory, Olfactory). Requires **Homebrew: Awareness Sense Channels** or the homebrew ruleset.
- **Roll**: keep mode. Requires **Advantage, Disadvantage & Emphasis Rolls**. See below.
- **Situational pills**: optional modifiers from talents, traits and conditions. Click to toggle; hover for details.
- **Gear**: carried equipment that could help. Click a chip to use the item.
- **Assistants**: friendly characters on the scene trained in the skill. Each adds +10, up to a cap, and is named on the card.
- **Passive Effects**: always-on modifiers, **already in the base**. Listed for reference only.
- **Extended Test**: tick and set a **Threshold** of total degrees of success. Progress carries between attempts.
- **Custom Modifier**: any extra modifier the GM calls for.

Notices:

- **Untrained**: the characteristic shows **Halved (untrained)**. An untrained advanced skill shows **Advanced skill — cannot attempt untrained.** Some skills let you choose an alternative characteristic.
- **Trying Again**: retrying a test in the same scene shows the rule and any cumulative penalty. It never blocks the roll.

## Roll or enter dice

- **Roll**: roll and post to chat.
- **Chat bubble**: **Post Target** to roll physical dice, or **Submit Roll** when you entered a manual roll.
- **Cancel**: close the dialog.

With **Allow manual roll input**, a **Manual Roll** section accepts **Two Dice** (**Tens** and **Units**) or a **Single Input**, and previews **SUCCESS** or **FAILURE** and degrees.

## Advantage, Disadvantage and Emphasis

A house rule enabled by **Advantage, Disadvantage & Emphasis Rolls**:

- **Normal**: one d100.
- **Advantage**: two d100, keep the lower.
- **Disadvantage**: two d100, keep the higher.
- **Emphasis**: two d100, keep the roll furthest from 50. On a tie the first roll stands.

The card shows both dice, for example "Advantage: rolled 23 and 71, kept 23". The dice tooltip strikes through the discarded die.

## The chat card

**Target side**: **Base**, one row per modifier with its source (Difficulty, Aim, Range, Assistance, a talent, gear), then **Target**. Hover a row for its derivation. A **Modifier cap (±60)** row shows raw and capped totals when the cap applied.

**Result side**:

- **Roll vs Target**.
- **Degrees of Success** or **Degrees of Failure**, with the formula, for example "3 DoS = 1 + 2 (roll 27 vs target 49)". Hover for an explanation.
- **Success** or **Failure**, and the **hit location** for a successful attack.
- The opponent's roll and result for opposed tests.

### Degrees

Degrees start at 1. Extra degrees depend on the line, unless the GM sets **Degrees of Success Calculation**:

- **Gen 1** (Dark Heresy 1e, Deathwatch, Rogue Trader): one per full 10 points between roll and target.
- **Gen 2** (Dark Heresy 2e, Black Crusade, Only War): the difference between the tens digits.

## Card buttons

- **Refund**: refund the ammo, Fate and other resources the action spent, after confirmation.
- **+1 DoS**: on a success, spend a Fate Point for one more Degree of Success.
- **Re-roll**: spend a Fate Point to re-roll. Talents and traits that grant re-rolls add their own buttons, which may be limited per encounter or session.

You can spend one Fate Point per roll unless the GM enables **Multiple Fate Burn Per Roll (Homebrew)**.
