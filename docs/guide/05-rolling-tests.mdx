# Rolling Tests

Every test in the system, whether a characteristic, skill, weapon attack or psychic power, goes through the same **roll dialog** and produces a chat card that shows exactly how the result was reached.

## Starting a roll

Click a characteristic or skill on the **Statistics** tab, or a weapon or power on the **Combat** tab. The roll dialog opens with your actor's name and portrait in its header.

## The roll dialog

### Target number and difficulty

The large number at the top is your **final target**: you succeed if the d100 roll is equal to or under it. A roll of 1 always succeeds and a roll of 100 always fails. Next to it is the difficulty button. Click it to pick a difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Trivial | +60 |
| Elementary | +50 |
| Simple | +40 |
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging (default) | +0 |
| Difficult | −10 |
| Hard | −20 |
| Very Hard | −30 |
| Arduous | −40 |
| Punishing | −50 |
| Hellish | −60 |

Under the target, a breakdown line shows **Base**, the difficulty, **Mods** (situational modifiers) and **Custom**, so you can see where the number comes from before you roll.

### Modifier sections

Only the sections that apply to the current test are shown.

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Skill uses | For skills with several distinct uses, pick the one you are attempting. Some uses ask you to pick a target. |
| **Test Variant** | Pick a sense channel (Visual, Auditory, Olfactory) for skills that declare one. Requires the **Homebrew: Awareness Sense Channels** setting or the homebrew ruleset; otherwise the dialog tells you the variants are disabled. |
| **Roll** (keep mode) | Normal, Advantage, Disadvantage or Emphasis. Only shown when the GM enables **Advantage, Disadvantage & Emphasis Rolls**. See below. |
| Situational pills | Optional bonuses and penalties from your talents, traits and conditions. Click a pill to switch it on or off. Hover to see when it applies. |
| **Gear** | Equipment you carry that could help this test. Click a chip to say you are using that item. |
| **Assistants** | One chip per friendly character on the scene who is trained in the skill. Each assistant you select adds +10, up to a cap. Selected assistants are named on the chat card. |
| **Passive Effects** | Always-on modifiers from talents, traits, effects and conditions. These are listed so you can see them, but they are **already included in the base** and cannot be switched off. |
| **Extended Test** | Tick to make this an extended test and set a **Threshold** of total degrees of success. Progress carries over between attempts. |
| **Custom Modifier** | Add any extra modifier the GM calls for. |

Other notices you may see:

- **Untrained skills**: the characteristic is shown as **Halved (untrained)**, and an untrained advanced skill shows **Advanced skill — cannot attempt untrained.** Where the skill allows alternatives, you can choose which characteristic it uses.
- **Trying Again**: if you retry a test in the same scene, a warning explains the rule and shows any cumulative penalty. It never blocks the roll.

### Rolling or entering your own dice

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Roll** | Rolls the dice and posts the result to chat. |
| Chat bubble | Posts the target to chat without rolling (**Post Target**), so you can roll physical dice. If you entered a manual roll, it submits that instead (**Submit Roll**). |
| **Cancel** | Closes the dialog. |

If the GM enables **Allow manual roll input**, a **Manual Roll** section appears. Enter physical dice either as **Two Dice** (separate **Tens** and **Units**) or as a **Single Input**. The dialog previews **SUCCESS** or **FAILURE** and the degrees before you submit.

### The ±60 modifier cap

The total of all modifiers is capped at +60 and −60. If the cap applies, the chat card shows a **Modifier cap (±60)** row with the raw total and the capped total.

## Advantage, Disadvantage and Emphasis

These house-rule modes are **off by default**. The GM turns them on with the world setting **Advantage, Disadvantage & Emphasis Rolls**. When on, the roll dialog shows a **Roll** selector:

| Mode | Dice | Kept result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | One d100 | That roll |
| Advantage | Two d100 | The lower roll (better, since you roll under the target) |
| Disadvantage | Two d100 | The higher roll |
| Emphasis | Two d100 | The roll furthest from 50, the more extreme result either way. On a tie, the first roll stands. |

The chat card shows both dice, with a note such as "Advantage: rolled 23 and 71, kept 23". In the dice tooltip, the discarded die is shown struck through.

## Reading the chat card

The card prints the whole calculation, not just the total.

### Target side

The card lists the **Base** value, then **one row per modifier** with its source (for example Difficulty, Aim, Range, Assistance, a talent or a piece of gear), then the final **Target**. **Hover over any modifier row** to see a tooltip explaining where that value came from. If the ±60 cap applied, a **Modifier cap (±60)** row shows the raw and capped totals.

### Result side

- **Roll vs Target**: your roll compared with the target.
- **Degrees of Success** or **Degrees of Failure**, with the formula underneath, for example "3 DoS = 1 + 2 (roll 27 vs target 49)". Hover the formula for an explanation.
- **Success** or **Failure**, and for successful attacks the **hit location**.
- For opposed tests, the opponent's roll and result.

### How degrees are counted

Degrees always start at 1 for a success or failure. Extra degrees depend on the game line, unless the GM forces one method with the **Degrees of Success Calculation** setting:

| Method | Lines (by default) | Extra degrees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gen 1 | Dark Heresy 1e, Deathwatch, Rogue Trader | One per full 10 points between roll and target |
| Gen 2 | Dark Heresy 2e, Black Crusade, Only War | The difference between the tens digits of roll and target |

## Fate Points and re-rolls on the card

The buttons under a result let you act on it:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Refund** | Refunds the ammo, Fate and other resources this action spent (asks for confirmation). |
| **+1 DoS** | On a success, spend a Fate Point to add one Degree of Success. |
| **Re-roll** | Spend a Fate Point to re-roll the test. Talents or traits that grant re-rolls add their own buttons, which may be limited per encounter or per session. |

By default you can only spend one Fate Point per roll. The GM can allow more with **Multiple Fate Burn Per Roll (Homebrew)**.
