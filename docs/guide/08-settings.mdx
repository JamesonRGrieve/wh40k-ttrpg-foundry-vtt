# Game settings reference

Open **Game Settings → Configure Settings → WH40K RPG**. Settings are **world** settings, set by the GM for everyone, unless marked **client** (per user and browser). **Reload** means Foundry reloads on change. Defaults are in parentheses.

## Game line and rules variants

- **Primary Game System** (Dark Heresy 2e; Reload): the world's line. Used for content viewed outside an actor, the token condition list, and actors created without a line.
- **DH2e Economy Ruleset** (Homebrew; Reload): **RAW (Influence + Requisition)** hides Throne Gelt. **Homebrew (Influence + Requisition + Throne Gelt)** adds Throne Gelt as street-level currency and starts Influence at 0 with no starting roll.
- **Degrees of Success Calculation** (**RAW (per game system)**): or force **Gen 1** (one degree per full 10 points of margin) or **Gen 2** (difference of the tens digits). Both start at 1.
- **Fatigue Model** (**Auto (per game system)**): or force for every actor **Halving** (fatigued characteristics halved, DH1/DH2), **Flat** (−10 to all tests, RT/DW/OW/BC) or **Condition** (two-tier Fatigued condition).
- **Multiple Fate Burn Per Roll (Homebrew)** (off): allow more than one Fate Point per roll, such as a re-roll plus +1 DoS.
- **Advantage, Disadvantage & Emphasis Rolls** (off): adds the keep-mode selector. See [Rolling Tests](?path=/docs/guide-rolling-tests--docs).
- **Homebrew Self-Targeting** (off): targeting yourself uses point-blank range and skips size, cover and target-state modifiers.
- **Homebrew: Awareness Sense Channels** (off): Awareness resolves per sense (Visual, Auditory, Olfactory). Sense bonuses apply to their channel only; blinding or deafening auto-fails the matching channel.
- **Higher Ground elevation band** (0): scene units the attacker must be above the target for the High Ground bonus. 0 means any height.
- **Black Crusade apotheosis Infamy** (100): Infamy needed when Corruption reaches 100 to become a Daemon Prince rather than a Chaos Spawn.

## Character creation and editing

- **Prompt to Build Unfinished Characters** (on): opening a character or NPC sheet with an incomplete origin path offers the builder.
- **Characteristic Offset** (0): added to the builder's base of 25. May be negative.
- **Point-Buy Pool** (60): points for point-buy, 1 per +1 above base.
- **Freeform Character Editing** (off): unlocks special abilities, malignancies, mutations, mental disorders and a character's own weapons and origin paths, and allows dropping origin path items on a character. Make lasting changes in the compendium.
- **Freeform Talent and Trait Creation** (off): shows a drop-to-add zone on the Talents and Traits panels. Off, these come through advancement.
- **NPCs advance like characters** (off; Reload): splits printed NPC characteristics into purchased advances, derives aptitudes and sets starting XP to match, so NPCs can advance. Printed values do not change.

## Rolls

- **Allow manual roll input** (off): shows manual dice entry in roll dialogs.
- **Simple Attack Rolls** (client; off; Reload): attacks roll a plain Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill test.
- **Simple Psychic Rolls** (client; off; Reload): powers roll a plain Willpower test.

## Combat automation

- **Require Active Combat to Attack** (on): attacks need an active encounter that includes the attacker.
- **Auto-roll Damage on Hit** (on): a successful weapon or psychic attack posts its damage card.
- **Auto-apply Damage to Target** (off): GM-resolved damage against a selected target applies automatically, including critical damage.
- **Auto Line of Sight & Cover** (off): checks scene walls for line of sight and full or half cover, and pre-selects cover. Low cover marked near the target also counts. Check results on your scenes.
- **Movement Automation** (**Full Automation**): enforces speeds and turn order in combat. **Display Only (no cost enforcement)** shows movement only. **No Automation** turns it off.
- **Active Effect Processing** (on; Reload): processes ongoing effects such as Fire or Blood Loss each turn.
- **Auto-roll Psychic Phenomena** (on): draws from Psychic Phenomena; 75 or more continues to Perils of the Warp. Off, the GM draws manually.
- **Enable Magazine Building** (off): shows **Build Clip** on weapons. **Reload** is unaffected.
- **Dead tokens become lootable piles** (off): a creature gaining Dead drops its carried gear as an Item Piles pile. Requires Item Piles. Talents, traits, skills, powers and bound items never drop.

## Content and compendiums

- **Hydrate Embedded Items From Compendiums on World Boot** (on): joins each embedded item's description and rules from its compendium in memory at load, without writing to the database. Off, items stay incomplete until their sheet opens.
- **Reconcile Origin-Path Grants on World Boot** (on): at load (GM only), re-applies origin paths so missing trained-skill grants repair themselves.
- **Compendium drops import a world Actor** (off): see [Other Tools](?path=/docs/guide-other-tools--docs). Player characters always import.

## In-game time

- **Campaign Inception Date (Imperial)** (0.000.001.M41): Day 0, as check.fraction.year.millennium, for example 5.420.816.M41.

## AI image generation

Connects to an OpenAI-compatible image service you provide. Nothing is generated without an endpoint. Also powers **Generate portrait (AI)**.

- **Auto-Generate Token Image** (**Never**): for actors dragged from a compendium: **Never**, **Only if the actor has no image**, **Ask each time** or **Always**.
- **Image Endpoint URL** (blank): API base URL; the system posts to `/v1/images/generations`.
- **Image Model** (blank): model name.
- **Image API Key** (blank): optional bearer token.
- **Image Size** (1024x1024).

## Maintenance

- **World Version** (1; Reload): migration tracking. Leave unchanged.

## Values set elsewhere

Stored as settings but edited in play:

- **Warband Subtlety Pool** (Dark Heresy 2e): the Subtlety stepper on any acolyte's sheet.
- **Celestial Bodies & Weather**: the world-time widget.
- Saved NPC combat presets and the campaign inception world-time stamp.
