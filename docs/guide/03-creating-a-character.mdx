# Creating a character

## Create the actor

Click **Create Actor** in the Actors sidebar and set:

- **Game System**: the actor's line.
- **Kind**: **Player Character**, **NPC** or a vehicle kind.
- **Name**: optional. Blank gives a default such as "New Dark Heresy 2e Player Character".

| Line | Kinds |
| --- | --- |
| Dark Heresy 2e, Only War, Deathwatch | Player Character, NPC, Land Vehicle, Aircraft |
| Dark Heresy 1e, Black Crusade | Player Character, NPC, Land Vehicle |
| Rogue Trader | Player Character, NPC, Land Vehicle, Aircraft, Voidcraft |

The line is fixed by actor type. To change a player character's line, use **Convert System** (see [Other Tools](?path=/docs/guide-other-tools--docs)).

## Open the Origin Path Builder

Click the **d20 icon** beside the portrait (**Setup Origin Path**).

A sheet whose origin path was never completed shows **Origin Path Incomplete** with **Build Now** and **Later**. Tick **Don't show this again for this character** to dismiss it. The GM can disable the prompt with **Prompt to Build Unfinished Characters**.

## Builder controls

- **Randomise Path**: fills empty slots with random choices, after confirmation.
- **Reset All**: clears all selections.
- **Guided** / **Free**: Guided follows the origin path chart and offers only valid next choices. Free ignores path requirements.
- **Export Path** / **Import Path**: save the path to a file or load one.

Click a step in the step tracker to jump to it. In Guided mode, later steps unlock as earlier ones complete.

## Steps by line

| Line | Origin steps | Optional | Extra steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Heresy 2e | Home World, Background, Role | Elite Advance | Characteristics, Equip Acolyte |
| Dark Heresy 1e | Home World, Career | — | Characteristics |
| Rogue Trader | Home World, Birthright, Lure of the Void, Trials and Travails, Motivation, Career | Lineage | Characteristics |
| Black Crusade | Race, Archetype, Pride, Disgrace, Motivation | — | Characteristics |
| Only War | Regiment, Speciality | — | Characteristics |
| Deathwatch | Chapter, Speciality | — | Characteristics |

In Rogue Trader you can build **Home World → Career** or **Career → Home World**. Changing direction resets your selections.

## Choose an origin

1. Pick an origin card. Badges mark choices, advanced options and XP cost.
2. Review the grants in the selection panel. Click a linked skill or talent to open it, or **View Details** for the full entry.
3. Under **Choices Required**, use **Make Choice** for each choice.
4. For rolls (wounds, Fate Points, starting Throne Gelt), use **Roll** or enter a value. When rolling, choose **Roll For Me** or **I'll Roll Myself**.
5. Click **Confirm Selection**, or **Select This Origin** in the details window.

**Show:** filters origins by provenance: **Official**, **Adapted**, **Homebrew**. World-created origins are marked **Authored in this world, not in a compendium**.

In Dark Heresy 2e, an origin that grants an aptitude you already have shows **Duplicate aptitude detected**. Use **Pick replacement** to choose another.

## Characteristics

Each characteristic starts at a base of 25 plus the GM's **Characteristic Offset**. Origin bonuses are added on top. **Advanced Mode** sets custom base values for non-human characters.

- **Point Buy**: each +1 above base costs 1 point from a pool set by **Point-Buy Pool** (default 60).
- **Roll**: roll 2d10 per characteristic and add it to the base. **Roll All** rolls every characteristic.
- **Roll Pool (HB)**: house rule. Roll a bank of 2d10 results (**Dice Rolls Bank**), then drag each onto a characteristic. Click a chip to type a value.

## Equip Acolyte (Dark Heresy 2e)

Pick starting gear from the armoury packs, up to a selection limit. Enter or roll your starting Influence under **Starting Resources** first. The step shows availability, plus Requisition and Throne Gelt costs when **DH2e Economy Ruleset** is Homebrew.

## Apply to the character

Click **Apply to Character**. The builder lists anything outstanding, such as pending choices, rolls or unassigned characteristics.

## Spend XP

Click **Advance** in the Overview tab's **Experience** panel to open **Character Advancement**, where you buy characteristic advances, skills, talents and psychic powers. It needs a career from the origin path; without one it shows **No Career Selected**.

- Dropping a talent on a character opens Character Advancement, because talents cost XP. **Freeform Talent and Trait Creation** shows a drop-to-add zone on the Talents and Traits panels.
- Dropping an origin path item on a character is refused unless **Freeform Character Editing** is on.
