# Sound Attribution

The attack-cue and status-visual sounds in this folder (#609) are all
**CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No credit is required; it
is given here anyway. Pack documents reference them as
`systems/wh40k-rpg/packs/sounds/<folder>/<file>`.

Kenney's own `License.txt` is kept beside each Kenney folder's files.

| Sources | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds), [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) | Kenney (www.kenney.nl) | CC0 1.0 |
| [100 CC0 SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx), [100 CC0 SFX #2](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2), [30 CC0 SFX loops](https://opengameart.org/content/30-cc0-sfx-loops) | rubberduck (OpenGameArt.org) | CC0 1.0 |

Files keep their original names. A few long ones are trimmed (re-encoded as Ogg
Vorbis with a short fade-out) so they suit a one-shot cue; the table says which.
